Privacy policy

rclone-backup. Last updated 26 October 2026.

What this app is

rclone-backup is a personal backup setup used by one person, its owner. It is not offered to the public.

What data it handles

  • Files from the owner's own Mac. They are encrypted on the Mac before upload.
  • The encrypted backups, stored in the owner's own Google Drive.
  • The Google sign-in token that lets it reach that Drive. It is kept only on the owner's Mac.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect data about anyone.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • It does not share, sell or send data to anyone other than Google Drive, where the owner's backups are kept.

How Google data is used

Access to Google Drive is used only to store the owner's backups and to read them back when files are restored.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's third-party access page, and the backups can be deleted from Google Drive.
